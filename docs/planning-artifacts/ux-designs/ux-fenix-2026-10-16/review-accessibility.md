# Accessibility review (inline)

**Verdict:** adequate — contrast and target sizes committed; three behavioral gaps.

- **high** New-order sound has no non-audio equivalent on staff phones when the app is in background. *Fix:* visual highlight + vibration on phones; service chat message remains the background channel.
- **medium** Price and order status update live without screen reader announcement. *Fix:* total and status regions announced politely (live region).
- **medium** Payment wait timer must not be the only indication of time left; give text "осталось N минут" and do not auto-close the screen on expiry without a message.
- **low** Tenor Sans uppercase with tracking is used only for short headings — keep max 3 words per heading line for readability.
